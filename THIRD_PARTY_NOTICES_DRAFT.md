# Third-party notices — draft

This repository contains no raw medical data, third-party source archives, model weights, checkpoints, renders, masks, or method outputs.

The dependency-light `benchmark_integrity` modules are first-party material and remain licence-pending. The final public release must document any runtime dependencies introduced by the image adapter and metric implementations.

The author manuscript and reading copy are included under `paper/`; their versions and
provenance are documented in `paper/README.md`. No third-party paper PDFs are bundled.

The author conference video and English captions are included under `presentation/`.
